Notes

Phone microphones aren't lab instruments, so expect a few dB of error. Readings above about 110 dB will run low because most phone mics clip. Avg is the energy average (Leq) since the last reset. The safe-time estimate follows NIOSH guidance: 85 dBA for 8 hours, halved for every 3 dB above that.
